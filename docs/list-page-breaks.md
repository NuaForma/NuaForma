# Explicit page breaks inside numbered paragraphs

The development candidate extends the existing `PdfPageBreak(style)` body-flow behavior to DOCX numbered paragraphs. A page boundary keeps one logical list item: its number appears once, subsequent text keeps the continuation indentation, and the next paragraph receives the next list ordinal. Leading page boundaries move the number with the first content; consecutive boundaries preserve deliberate blank pages. A trailing boundary does not invent another list item.

Twelve genuine Word controls cover leading, middle, trailing and consecutive breaks, plus empty first/last list items, on A4 and Letter. The final boundary does not create an unused empty page, and paragraph-after spacing does not move onto a page opened by an ending boundary. Five additional Word research controls confirm final-boundary behavior for plain and numbered paragraphs. `python3 tools/pdf-evaluation/create_list_page_break_fixtures.py <fresh-directory>` reproduces the source package parts without overwriting earlier controls. Independent content, page-count, marker-position and visual review precede initial native golden images. Existing golden files remain protected. Native/profile, Playground, corpus and complete-build qualification are recorded below.

Existing rejections for page breaks in frames, cells, page bands, notes, paragraph keep rules and wrapped-frame combinations remain in force. No public API, package, dependency, Playground control or release changes.

All twelve Word/native controls, covering 34 page pairs, passed independent per-page content, numbering, paper, marker-position and visual checks before the initial native PNG references were authored. Maximum measured position differences are 0.060 point horizontally and 0.068 point vertically, within the unchanged 0.21-point bounds. The maintained golden/profile matrix passes 24 PDFs / 73 pages in the combined normal build. Actual Playground requests produced 36 PDFs / 102 pages across standard PDF and PDF/A-1b/2b/3b, with per-page Word content and exact native provider pixels at 96 dpi without masks.

The local 250-document run converts 22 documents successfully, including the newly supported six-page list document. All sources remain unchanged. The previous 21 successful conversions retain their text and image resources: 49 pages remain pixel-identical and nine pages have the expected spacing or image-flow changes. One report's eight images repaginate from fifteen to fourteen pages with their decoded bytes, dimensions and counts preserved, without overlap or physical clipping. Original batch outputs and all 374 previously tracked golden PNGs remain unchanged. Corpus conversion coverage does not establish pixel-perfect Word fidelity; original Word exports still show other layout differences in the representative reports. The complete combined normal build passed on 2026-11-19 with 1,948 JVM tests, no failures, errors or skipped tests. The final source inputs stayed unchanged throughout verification.
