# Paragraph backgrounds and tight automatic spacing

Current source adds optional `PdfParagraph.background` and immutable `withBackground(color)` to the shared PDF model. Null clears the decoration; white is opaque. Existing constructors and copy methods retain their signatures and preserve the setting. A later ODT adapter can reuse it in the same artifact. No package or dependency was added.

The DOCX adapter resolves solid paragraph shading independently from run shading and highlighting. Paragraph styles inherit their fill; direct `nil` retains an inherited fill, while `clear` with automatic fill removes it. RGB and the existing bounded theme resolver remain applicable. Automatic foreground uses the paragraph fill unless an opaque run fill takes precedence; explicit text colors remain explicit. Highlighting affects its own background, independently of that foreground decision.

Paragraph decoration follows hanging/ordinary indentation, border spacing, wrapping and pagination. Adjacent shaded paragraphs with matching visible horizontal bounds and border formatting can fill their actual intervening spacing, even with different colors. Different indentation can therefore keep fills separate; a first-line offset that preserves the same decoration bounds does not. Connections resolve after page-side offsets and only between fragments occupying the same canvas; they do not bridge page boundaries. Fills paint before text and inline objects, remain artifacts and retain searchable embedded-font content. Run backgrounds and opaque table-cell fills keep their own ordering.

`PdfAutomaticLineSpacing` now accepts finite multiples from `1/240` through `10`, including Word values such as `213/240`, `220/240` and `228/240`. Multiples below one reduce both line advance and baseline offset. Glyphs can overlap as in Word; physical ink checks remain active after placement. The minimum physical automatic line height remains one point, and the existing page, frame, text and resource limits remain. Zero, negative, nonfinite and out-of-range multiples reject. Smaller line multiples do not newly qualify compressed standalone inline objects or unsupported complex-script shaping.

## Verification

`python3 tools/pdf-evaluation/create_paragraph_formatting_fixtures.py <fresh-directory>` creates inert A4/Letter controls and refuses an existing output directory. Word references are authored separately with macro/link updates disabled and unchanged source hashes. Initial native golden images require independent visual review; existing golden images are protected and must not be refreshed to accommodate a regression.

The controls cover reduced line advances/baselines, mixed sizes, inherited spacing, page breaks, solid paragraph fills, clear/nil inheritance, automatic/explicit ink, indentation, wrapping, border padding, table cells with ordinary/zero margins, page bands and one shaded paragraph spanning pages. Cell decoration is clipped to its containing physical cell after final placement. Patterned shading and paragraph backgrounds beside floating body images remain unsupported. Table-level shading and the larger floating-frame compatibility work are separate capabilities; this source change does not claim them.

The deliberate half-line overlap controls compare full text in logical content order: geometric text sorting can interleave overlapping glyph rows. Marker positions and foreground colors remain independently checked against Word, and native golden images still require an exact pixel match. Ordinary controls retain positional extraction.

Local qualification on 2026-11-18 passed the full build with 1,891 tests, including 903 DOCX/PDF and 380 shared PDF tests, without failures or skipped tests. The 26 independently exported Word controls cover A4 and Letter; their 30 initial native golden pages are protected against later replacement. Marker differences from Word stay within 0.14 point horizontally and 0.181 point vertically. Existing golden files were not replaced.

The actual Playground accepted 64 PDF requests across standard PDF and PDF/A-1b, 2b and 3b, with 74 pages retaining full Word-reference content and matching the provider's native reference pixels at 96 dpi without masks. Unsupported patterned shading and automatic line heights below one physical point returned precise failures without PDF output. The local 250-document corpus progressed from 17 to 18 complete conversions; all previous 29 successful pages retained identical text and pixels. Six tight-spacing and seven solid-shading first blockers were removed, with twelve of those documents exposing another existing limitation. Two patterned-shading failures are now detected earlier with a more specific source location. The newly successful four-page document was visually reviewed; this corpus check does not claim Word fidelity for its source.

The Playground uses the same provider and needs no separate control. Previously published artifacts remain unchanged. Qualification results belong to the source delivery; no release is authorized by this work.
