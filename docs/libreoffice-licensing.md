# LibreOffice distribution checklist

LibreOffice was selected for the early optional PDF adapter on 2026-10-12 and is packaged in the locally qualified service/runtime images. The user approved its additional licensing obligations. This document defines the remaining candidate-specific distribution gate, not a claim that the local images have a completed redistribution audit or are publicly available. It supplements [THIRD_PARTY.md](../THIRD_PARTY.md).

## License boundary

NuaForma's independently written engine remains Apache-2.0. LibreOffice is a separately executed program; its covered files and third-party components retain their respective licenses. Do not copy LibreOffice source into engine files or relabel an entire combined image as Apache-2.0. Private use and a hosted service alone do not trigger MPL distribution duties, but supplying a renderer binary or image to another party does. Other components must be reviewed independently. See [LibreOffice's licensing page](https://www.libreoffice.org/licenses/) and the [Mozilla MPL FAQ](https://www.mozilla.org/en-US/MPL/2.0/FAQ/).

For distributed MPL-covered binaries, provide the corresponding source under MPL and tell recipients how to obtain it. Keep existing notices. If covered files are modified and distributed, include those modifications in the offered source. Independently written separate files need not be relicensed merely because they form a larger work. These requirements follow [MPL-2.0 sections 3.1–3.4](https://www.mozilla.org/en-US/MPL/2.0/); they are not a substitute for obligations under other component licenses.

## Before distributing any renderer or image

- Pin and record the exact renderer version/build, package origin, checksums and, for an image, immutable digest and platform. Inventory the final image, not just the packages explicitly installed by a build script. Include the base OS, libraries, dictionaries, fonts and optional extensions actually present.
- Collect the installation's LICENSE and NOTICE files and package copyright/license records without stripping upstream notices. Retain original license text, attribution, disclaimers and any required notices for each included component. Map these materials to the actual package inventory; a link to LibreOffice's general licensing page is not a complete bundled notice set.
- Identify source obligations for every component. Preserve the matching LibreOffice source and any distribution patches/build materials required by its applicable licenses. Provide recipients with clear, working source-access instructions in the distributed artifact. Verify retrieval of the exact matching source rather than assuming that a mutable upstream download link will remain available. Archive the source/materials used to satisfy these obligations; additional licenses may require different source or offer arrangements.
- If covered renderer files are modified, inventory the modifications and include them in the corresponding source. Prefer an unmodified renderer and keep NuaForma's original compatibility-copy implementation separate; transforming a customer's temporary document is not modifying LibreOffice's source.
- Review all fonts and dictionaries separately. Do not redistribute private interoperability fonts such as the workstation's Arial copies. Retain permitted bundled font notices and naming conditions. Request-supplied fonts are private per-generation resources, not automatically distributable runtime assets; enforce technical embedding permissions without requiring extra ownership declarations.
- Include the final component inventory, upstream notices and source-access instructions in the image/package itself and link them from its usage documentation. Make clear which files are NuaForma, LibreOffice and other components. Ensure extraction/inspection works without starting an application service.
- Record the review against the final immutable artifact and check it again after package, base-image, font or renderer changes. Do not publish an image with an incomplete inventory, missing notices or unavailable corresponding source.

No distribution is authorized by this checklist itself. Building/verifying a future runtime and publishing it are separate workflow actions. A caller supplying their own LibreOffice installation does not make NuaForma the redistributor of that installation; explain the prerequisite accurately and do not claim that bundling it elsewhere avoids obligations.
