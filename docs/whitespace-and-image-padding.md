## Text-node whitespace

Ordinary Word `w:t` nodes trim XML S characters (space, tab, CR and LF) at their edges when `xml:space` is absent or `default`. Explicit `preserve` keeps those characters. Internal characters and qualified nonbreaking Unicode spaces retain their existing semantics. Empty text nodes contribute no replacement space; the paragraph's normal mark fallback still supplies an empty paragraph when needed. Original whitespace attributes, namespaces and structure are checked before typed binding.

They cover leading/trailing spaces, multiple internal spaces, all-space runs, style boundaries and wrapping. ## Inline picture extents

DOCX `wp:inline/wp:effectExtent` reserves space around the raster. Left padding moves the pixels and right padding extends the following advance. Top and bottom padding contribute to the automatic inline box; bottom padding also moves the pixels above the baseline. Image bytes and declared visible dimensions remain unchanged. Exact leading retains its fixed line height. The DOCX adapter selects line clipping: Word clips protruding inline picture pixels to that exact line box, including inside cells. The shared model defaults to unclipped painting so other adapters choose their own policy. Visible pixels are checked after final physical placement and clipping; original PNG validation and resource limits still apply to fully clipped images.

Four unqualified integer EMU attributes, `l`, `t`, `r` and `b`, are required together. The source qualification bounds each nonnegative extent at 14,400 points. The shared image and its padding together must remain within the existing 14,400-point layout dimension. Missing, foreign, malformed, duplicate and out-of-range source constructs reject without partial output. Actual drawing effects remain source rejections.
