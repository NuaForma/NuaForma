# Anchored images and rectangular wrapping

Roadmap part 10, issue [#57](https://github.com/NuaForma/NuaForma/issues/57). This is the qualified bounded source profile; CI and integration-branch gates remain delivery requirements. No release is published by this qualification.

The shared model associates immutable images with paragraphs instead of inserting independent floating blocks. Anchors follow their paragraph across explicit and automatic page breaks. Rectangular exclusions narrow individual text regions, including both sides of a central image, and restore the full width below it. Page-relative positions remain fixed on the anchor page. Source crops are clipped to explicit physical output dimensions; source pixel resolution does not change page geometry. Foreground images paint after text, as requested by the supported source policy.

The deterministic `build_anchored_image_fixtures.py` inputs cover left/right alignment, largest-side and both-side wrapping, top/bottom wrapping, page anchoring, near-bottom paragraph relocation, US Letter, asymmetric crop and a multi-page paragraph. `renderOwnEngineAnchoredImages` renders these through the production reader and shared engine. Genuine Microsoft Word exports use `export_word.ps1`; `compare.py --word-bounds` records source/export provenance and compares every page without alignment, masks or changed-content exemptions. Local references are retained outside Git in the `57-anchored-images-20261026` evidence directory.

Engine tests check actual line/image geometry, word preservation, anchor movement, explicit breaks, cropping pixels, resource/page limits and concurrent reuse. Provider tests additionally exercise template processing and genuine independent standard/PDF/A validation. Two new reviewed engine-only golden pages cover wrapping and crop; the previous 17 reference PNGs retain their original hashes. Normal tests never replace golden resources.

The final ten genuine Word exports cover fourteen page pairs. Every page preserves normalized text, word order, wrapping and pagination. Maximum word-start/end/top differences are 0.30/0.34/0.13 points; all page comparisons were visually reviewed. The paragraph-bound top/bottom image and its anchor move together to page two, while the long both-sided paragraph continues over four pages with one image occurrence. The no-wrap case intentionally paints over body text in both Word and the engine; rectangular wrapping cases avoid the image. The source/export hashes and per-page differences remain in each comparison report. No masks or accepted-content differences were used.

This profile rejects tight/through wrapping, behind-text images, overlapping floating image layers, arbitrary rotation/flips, nonempty stretch-fill rectangles, and anchors in cells/page bands. Wrapped keep groups, lists, tabs, mixed inline objects, RTL wrapping and intersecting body tables remain unqualified. Unbreakable words that exceed the selected region reject rather than overflow. Existing source/paragraph/image/page budgets and PDF/A-1b transparency restrictions remain in force. No dependency, module, Playground control, release workflow or publication identifier changes.

Vertical exclusion bounds must start at or below the anchor paragraph and end inside its body page. A picture cannot retroactively displace text already laid out above its anchor; such coordinates reject instead of overlapping preceding content.
